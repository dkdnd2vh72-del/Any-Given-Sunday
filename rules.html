Season Mulligan

Each team will get 1 mulligan for the season. Say you forgot to set your roster or a player gets hurt on the first play of the game — you are able to use a mulligan and substitute a player in.

  • Substitution of a player must occur prior to the end of the 1st quarter.
  • You may only substitute a player who is on your roster 24 hours before the start of their game.
  • The game of the player you use as a substitute must not have started.
  • The player you pick as the substitute must be announced in the group chat and HAS to play.

Trade Counteroffers

Whenever a trade is accepted in the league, all teams have the ability to intercept the trade and propose a better offer to either team. Only one interception is allowed.

Example — Doug trades Mahomes to Dre for Derrick Henry. Vick wants Mahomes, so he offers Doug CMC. Doug would rather get CMC, so he accepts Vick's offer of Mahomes for CMC.

Since Vick intercepted the trade, the Mahomes for CMC trade is not eligible for counteroffers.

Waiver Adds

Each player added to your team costs $1 of real money. The money will be due at the end of the regular season.

The money from the waiver adds — up to $100 — will be used to cover costs associated with the league, such as shipping the trophy, adding new plaques each year for the champion, or even replacing the trophy if it breaks.

If the transaction pot goes over $100, the total amount over goes toward the team with the highest scoring week in the season — playoffs included.

Teams are allowed to trade their "debt" to other teams if both parties accept the terms.

FAAB Trading

FAAB is allowed to be included in trades as long as it is announced in the league chat.

Keepers

  • You may only keep a player drafted in the 5th round or later.
  • You may only keep a player you drafted.
  • The keeper is worth a draft pick that is one round higher than you drafted them last year (Rd 5 last = 4th round draft pick this year).
  • If you make me change your keeper — meaning after you tell me who it is initially — the penalty is that your keeper is now worth TWO rounds higher than you drafted them last.
  • Deadline to submit keeper is 9/6/2026.
  • YOU DO NOT HAVE TO USE A KEEPER IF YOU DO NOT WANT TO. THIS MEANS YOU WILL RETAIN ALL DRAFT PICKS.
  • A player may only be kept 1 time.
