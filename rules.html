Season Mulligan

Each team will get 1 mulligan for the season. Say you forgot to set your roster or a player gets hurt on the first play of the game — you are able to use a mulligan and substitute a player in.

Rules of the Mulligan

  • Substitution of a player must occur prior to the end of the 1st quarter.
  • You may only substitute a player who is on your roster 24 hours before the start of their game.
  • The game of the player you use as a substitute must not have started.
  • The player you pick as the substitute must be announced in the group chat and HAS to play.

Keepers

  • You may only keep a player drafted in the 5th round or later.
  • You may only keep a player you drafted.
  • The keeper is worth a draft pick that is one round higher than you drafted them last year (Rd 5 last = 4th round draft pick this year).
  • If you make me change your keeper — meaning after you tell me who it is initially — the penalty is that your keeper is now worth TWO rounds higher than you drafted them last.
  • Deadline to submit keeper is 9/6/2026.
  • YOU DO NOT HAVE TO USE A KEEPER IF YOU DO NOT WANT TO. THIS MEANS YOU WILL RETAIN ALL DRAFT PICKS.
  • A player may only be kept 1 time.
